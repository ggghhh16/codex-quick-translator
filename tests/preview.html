Understanding monetary policy

Central banks use interest rates to influence borrowing, spending, and inflation. Higher rates make borrowing more expensive.

The bank raised interest rates to curb inflation.

This decision can slow consumer demand. In this context, the word “bank” refers to the central bank, rather than the edge of a river.

Reading between the lines

A single sentence becomes clearer when read alongside the article's main argument. Translation should preserve this relationship.
